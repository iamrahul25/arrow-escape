ArrowEscape

Clear every arrow. Find the order.

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points, unless another arrow is in the way. Work out the right order to clear the whole board before you run out of hearts. Simple to pick up, and it gets wonderfully tangled as the boards grow.

How to play

One tap per move. The trick is finding the order.

Tap an arrow

It slides along its path and out of the board in the direction its head points.

Don't collide

If another arrow is in the way, it bumps and you lose a heart. You have three per level.

Clear the board

Free every arrow to win. Finish without losing a heart to earn all three stars.

Features

A clean, relaxing puzzle game that grows from a quick warm-up into a real brain teaser.

🧩

249 handcrafted levels

Start on a tiny 4×4 grid and work your way up to huge 40×40 boards packed with arrows.

❤️

Custom shapes

Boards shaped like hearts, stars, rings, trees, mushrooms and more, each one a fresh puzzle to untangle.

💡

Hints when you're stuck

A hint highlights an arrow that can escape safely, so you can keep going without spoiling the whole board.

🔍

Pinch to zoom

Zoom and pan around the biggest boards so every arrow is easy to see and tap.

🛠️

Level Generator

Make your own levels from preset shapes, cells you draw, or a picture from your gallery, then play them instantly.

📳

Feels great to play

Smooth escape animations, a little shake when arrows collide and gentle haptic feedback you can switch off.
