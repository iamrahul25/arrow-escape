Delete Account & Data

Arrow Escape, by Rahul Kumar · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Arrow Escape has no accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in, so there is no account to delete. We don't keep any of your data on a server. Everything (your completed levels, stars, best scores and settings) is saved only on your own device, and you can erase it yourself at any time using the steps below.

What data exists

The only data the game keeps is stored locally on your device:

  • Levels completed, stars earned, best move counts and the furthest level unlocked in each suite
  • The level suite you played last
  • Your settings (sound, haptics, grid dots and developer mode)

Pictures you choose in the Level Generator are not saved by the game. We never receive any of this, and we hold no personal information about you. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

Choose whichever option suits you. Deleted progress can't be recovered.

Option 1: Reset progress inside the game

Erases your completed levels, stars and best scores, and puts your settings back to their defaults.

  1. Open Arrow Escape.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the home screen.
  3. Tap Reset Progress.
  4. Tap Reset to confirm.

Option 2: Clear the app's storage (Android)

Erases everything the game has saved, including any temporary files. The game starts fresh next time you open it.

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Arrow Escape.
  3. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly between phone makers and Android versions.

Option 3: Uninstall the game

Uninstalling Arrow Escape removes the app and all of its saved data from your device. On Android, press and hold the app icon and tap Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play Store.

Copies you made yourself

  • Levels you copied or saved: if you used the Level Generator to copy a level to your clipboard or save it through the share sheet, delete it from wherever you pasted or saved it. These files only contain the board layout.
  • Your photos: the game never changes or deletes pictures in your gallery. Pictures you picked for the Level Generator stay in your gallery until you delete them yourself.
  • Device backups: if Google backup is turned on, your device may have backed up the game's data. You can manage or delete these backups in your device or Google account settings. We don't have access to them.

Requests to the developer

Because we don't collect or store any data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request is needed. If you still have questions, or want written confirmation, email us and we'll reply as soon as possible.
