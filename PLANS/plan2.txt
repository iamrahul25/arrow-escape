### One important improvement

I'd actually use **path points + a generated arrowhead**, rather than storing `direction + length`. That will let you create levels like the screenshot:

```text
      ┌───────────────→
      │
  ┌───┘
  │
  │       ┌─────────┐
  │       │         │
  └───────┘         └──────→
```

while keeping the **same game engine**. This will also make creating future Level 4–100 much easier.
